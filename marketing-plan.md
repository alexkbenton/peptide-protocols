# Peptide Protocols — Digital Marketing Plan

**Goal:** Build an owned audience now so the future Shop launch has buyers ready.
**Constraints:** Faceless (no filming, no on-camera talent), $500–2,000/mo budget, mostly hands-off.
**Prepared:** June 2026

---

## 1. The strategic frame (read this first)

Your instinct — short-form video on TikTok — is correct. It's the cheapest way to reach the performance/wellness audience. But two realities shape everything below:

**Reality 1: Auto-reposting other people's viral clips will not work.**
It violates TikTok/IG/YouTube terms (leads to shadowbans and takedowns), it builds you no owned audience, and it's legally exposed (copyright). The automatable, *safe* version is to clip **your own or licensed source content** and auto-distribute it. That's the engine this plan is built on.

**Reality 2: Peptides are a high-risk content category.**
TikTok prohibits selling many peptide-adjacent products outright (testosterone boosters, fat burners, weight-loss/appetite products, sexual enhancers) and heavily suppresses unsubstantiated health claims. Disease/cure claims are banned by federal rule. This means two things: (a) your social accounts are *fragile* — you can lose one overnight — and (b) you should treat social as **rented land** and your email list as the **asset you own**. The whole funnel below pushes toward the email gate you've already built, precisely because it survives a platform ban.

So the plan is: **faceless educational content → distributed automatically across 3 platforms → drives to your email gate → warms a list for the Shop launch.**

---

## 2. The content engine (faceless, batchable)

Three formats, all producible without a camera, ranked by effort-to-payoff:

**A. AI-voiceover educational shorts (primary).**
Script → AI voiceover → stock/AI b-roll → animated captions. 20–45 seconds. Topics: "What is BPC-157?", "3 myths about [peptide]", "How researchers think about [mechanism]". Framed as *education and information*, never as medical advice or product pitch. This is the bread-and-butter, fully automatable format.

**B. Carousel / photo-mode posts (lowest risk, lowest effort).**
Text-on-image slides — myth vs. fact, protocol-at-a-glance, glossary terms. TikTok photo mode + Instagram carousels. These rarely trigger health-content moderation because there's no spoken claim, and they screenshot/save well (which the algorithm rewards). Great for steady, safe reach.

**C. Repurposed long-form clips (scales once you have source material).**
If you record audio-only explainers or a faceless screen-share walkthrough of a protocol page, one 20-minute recording becomes 10–15 clips via an AI clipper. No face required — voiceover + screen + captions.

**Content sourcing without a creator team:** you already own a content library — your protocol pages. Each protocol = a week of scripts (overview, each compound, the "why," the cautions). A freelance scriptwriter (~$15–25/script on Upwork/Fiverr) can turn your existing pages into 20–30 scripts in one batch, then the tools do the rest. That's the one place a few dollars of human input dramatically raises quality.

> Note on paid ads: TikTok/Meta routinely **reject** peptide and health-claim ads. Don't plan a paid-acquisition strategy around them. The reserve is better spent on (a) boosting your *own* organic winners where allowed, and (b) more/better content.

## 4. The funnel

```
Short-form content (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
        │  bio link + on-screen CTA: "Full protocol → free at the link"
        ▼
   Age gate (peptideprotocols.us)
        │  email + 18+ confirm  →  Mailchimp
        ▼
   Email nurture sequence
        │  weekly value, protocol deep-dives, "what's coming"
        ▼
   Shop launch  →  warm list ready to buy on day one
```

The content's job is **not** to sell — it's to get a click to the gate. The gate's job is to capture the email. The email list is what you're actually building. By launch, a 5–10k engaged list is worth more than any single viral video.

**Set up now so the list is ready:** finish the Mailchimp env vars (currently unconfigured per your project notes) and build a simple 4–5 email welcome sequence. Without this, the traffic leaks.

## 5. Cadence & how "hands-off" it really is

Honest version: this is **~90% automated, not 100%.** Plan on **2–4 hours/week** of light oversight. Fully unattended bot accounts in a health niche get flagged and don't build trust.

- **Daily (automated):** 1–2 shorts + 3–4 carousels/week auto-post across all 3 platforms on a schedule.
- **Weekly (you/team, ~2–4 hrs):** approve the next batch of scripts, skim comments/DMs for moderation flags, glance at which posts popped, queue a boost on any winner.
- **Monthly (~1 hr):** review metrics, kill formats that aren't working, double down on what is.

---

## 6. Compliance guardrails (non-negotiable for this niche)

These keep your accounts alive:

- **Never** make disease, cure, treatment, or weight-loss claims ("cures," "treats," "burns fat," "fixes"). These are federally prohibited and the fastest path to a ban.
- Frame everything as **education and information**, not advice. Use "research suggests," "in studies," "for informational purposes."
- Keep a visible disclaimer in bio and on content: *educational only, not medical advice, not FDA-evaluated.*
- **Do not use TikTok Shop** to sell peptides — many are in prohibited categories. Sell through your own site only.
- No before/after transformation imagery, no "clinically proven" without documentation.
- Because accounts are fragile: **run 2–3 accounts per platform** from day one, keep all source files, and treat any single account as expendable. The email list is the backup.

## 7. 90-day rollout

**Days 1–14 — Foundation**
Configure Mailchimp env vars + build welcome email sequence. Set up TikTok/IG/YouTube accounts (2–3 each) with compliant bios + link. Commission first batch of 20–30 scripts from your protocol library. Set up ReelMoney/AutoReels + OpusClip.

**Days 15–45 — Launch the engine**
Begin auto-posting: ~5 pieces/week per platform across formats A, B, C. Don't chase virality yet — you're gathering data on what the algorithm and audience reward. Track click-through to the gate and signups.

**Days 46–90 — Optimize & scale**
Cut the bottom-performing format. Double the output of the winner. Start light paid boosts on organic winners (where allowed). Goal by day 90: a repeatable weekly batch process and a **measurably growing email list.**

---

## 8. What success looks like (metrics)

The vanity metric is views. The metric that matters for your goal is **email signups**, because email has the lowest acquisition cost (~$25 vs ~$120 for paid social) and it's the asset that survives a platform ban.

Track weekly: signups, cost-per-signup, click-through from bio to gate, and which content format drives the most signups (not the most views). Optimize for signups.

---

## 9. Straight talk — what won't work

- **Pure auto-reposting of influencer clips:** bans + zero owned audience. Don't.
- **100% unattended:** budget the few hours/week.
- **Paid peptide ads:** mostly rejected; don't build the plan on them.
- **Selling via TikTok Shop:** prohibited categories; sell on your own site.
- **Optimizing for views:** views you can't convert are worthless for a launch. Optimize for the list.

The version of your original idea that *does* work: an automated pipeline that clips **your own** content and blasts it everywhere, funneling to an email list you own. That's what this builds.
